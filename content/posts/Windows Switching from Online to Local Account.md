So I signed in to my Microsoft Outlook account `burberryswag@outlook.com` on the Outlook app and it accidentally signed in to my Local Account on my Windows 11 machine.

![Imgur](https://i.imgur.com/TiL2dxt.png)


To fix so:
- `Settings` -> `Email & Accounts` -> `Accounts used by Other apps`

![Image_2](https://i.imgur.com/7hqhVZF.png)

Then you will be prompted to remove such email and Boom you are back to Local Account.
